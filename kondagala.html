The Tea Estate Peak

Kondagala, also known as Kondagala Rock, is a hidden hiking destination located above the historic Loolkandura Tea Estate in Sri Lanka’s Kandy District. It offers a quiet trail experience with panoramic views, making it a peaceful alternative to crowded tourist viewpoints. The hike to Kondagala typically begins from the upper area of Loolkandura, near historical spots like James Taylor’s seat and the old cabin remains. The trail is not always clearly marked, so it's advisable to ask locals or rely on instinct. The summit provides a large flat rock plateau with wide panoramic views across the surrounding mountains, including Hanthana and the Gampola region. Despite being a relatively short hike, Kondagala offers rewarding views and a peaceful estate setting.
