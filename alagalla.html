The Fortress In The Sky

The Alagalla Mountain Range, also known as the Potato Range, is a significant geographical feature in Sri Lanka, located at the border of the Central and Sabaragamuwa Provinces. This mountain range, standing at an elevation of 1,140 meters, has a rich history as a natural defense against invasions by the Portuguese, Dutch, and English during the Kandyan Kingdom's time. The range is surrounded by other notable mountains and is accessible via Sri Lanka's railway system. It is a popular destination for hikers, offering trails surrounded by misty forests and panoramic views. The area is also known for its biodiversity and is a region with different climatic and environmental conditions.
