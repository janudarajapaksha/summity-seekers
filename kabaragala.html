The Giant Of Dolosbage

kabaragala is the highest peak in the dolosbage mountain range. kwnon for its massive rock cliff that resembles skin of a kabara,this hike takes you through lush tea estates and dense pine forests.

Once you reach summit, the 360-degrees view is legendary you can see the Bible Rock,Asupini alla,and even the distant sri pada(adam's peak) on a clear day.The mist here is like nowhere in sri lanka
